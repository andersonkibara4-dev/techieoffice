| Switch Type | Feel | Sound Profile | Best Used For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Linear (Red/Yellow)** | Smooth actuation | Quiet thock | Fast gaming & quiet offices |
| **Tactile (Brown/Banana)** | Subtle tactile bump | Medium sound | Programming & typing accuracy |
| **Clicky (Blue)** | Sharp bump with click | Loud click | Dedicated home office typing |
