## Streamlining Your Workstation

A chaotic desk breeds a chaotic mind. By introducing multi-functional accessories, you can reclaim valuable surface area while maintaining immediate access to your primary tools.

## 1. Store Headphones Off the Desk Surface

Leaving wireless headphones lying flat on your desk creates unnecessary clutter and risks scratching the earcups. An under-desk or low-profile aluminum headphone stand gives your audio gear a dedicated, elevated home.

## Final Checklist for a Minimalist Workspace

* Keep primary touchpoints within arm's reach.
* Route all active power cords through behind-desk channels.
* Clear the central pad area for your keyboard and mouse only.
